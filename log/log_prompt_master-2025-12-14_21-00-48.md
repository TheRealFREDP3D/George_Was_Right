**Set of Prompts for Recent Event Illustration: "Venezuela: Freedom on the Net 2024 Country Report"**

1. **Backdrop & Setting:**
   - Depict a digital landscape of Venezuela with a shadowy overlay of government surveillance.
   - Show a network of interconnecting digital lines, symbolizing the internet, with sections visibly censored or blocked.

2. **Key Figures & Actions:**
   - Illustrate shadowy figures representing government officials controlling computer screens displaying censored content.
   - Include imagery of people trying to access blocked websites, showcasing frustration and helplessness.

3. **Symbolic Elements:**
   - Incorporate symbols of chains or padlocks over digital devices, representing restricted freedom.
   - Use visuals of a cracked globe or fractured digital map of Venezuela to symbolize the fragmentation and manipulation of information.

4. **Mood & Tone:**
   - Utilize dark, muted colors to convey a sense of oppression and control.
   - Create an atmosphere of tension and surveillance, with eyes or cameras subtly integrated into the background.

**Set of Prompts for '1984' Theme Illustration: Surveillance and Information Control**

1. **Backdrop & Setting:**
   - Create a dystopian cityscape reminiscent of Orwell's '1984', with towering structures and omnipresent screens.
   - Include a large, foreboding image of the Ministry of Truth in the background, exuding authority and control.

2. **Key Figures & Actions:**
   - Illustrate a crowd of uniformed citizens walking in unison, with expressions of conformity and obedience.
   - Show a central figure with a blurred face, symbolizing the suppression of identity and individuality.

3. **Symbolic Elements:**
   - Incorporate the iconic "Big Brother is Watching You" poster, modified to include modern-day surveillance technology like drones and security cameras.
   - Use visual motifs like clocks with broken hands or newspapers with crossed-out headlines to signify altered truths and time distortion.

4. **Mood & Tone:**
   - Employ a grayscale color palette with stark contrasts to evoke a sense of bleakness and control.
   - Create an atmosphere of fear and obedience, with subtle hints of rebellion, such as a small, hidden flower or torn posters.

These prompts aim to visually communicate the complex socio-political commentary on surveillance and information control, drawing connections between current events and Orwell's timeless themes in '1984'.
